
> [!info] In universe
> The world of Teras was a highly advanced world until the merging of the overlapping planes of existence the Shadowfell, Feywild and Material Plane. It all started with a bright flash of green and black flashing across the sky and ground then suddenly creatures. Some torn some otherworldly, the ones with the aura of bloods and tears started ravaging anything close by. The sudden disappearance of people, structures and then the sudden emergence of landscapes, buildings, and creatures merged into each other if unlucky enough to overlap. Not all of the planes were merged but certain central locations named Dimensional Portals, spilled over into the material. Then after the first merge panic the flash went away, only to repeat and appear every year. Due to the mixing planes a new way of telling time had to be invented due to time changing. The Fey Sun marks the first of a new year.


> [!info] Out of Universe
> Cult-Veiled Concord sought magic to break free from death and the Raven Queen. using the Feywild magics to try and fight against an inevitable death of the old emperor or the grandfather of Thulk founder of [[Kret - Capital of Esther]]. After taking a portal into the Feywild with the king and soldiers. Trying necromantic magic in the Feywild created a disturbance where the mixed emotions of fighting fate of the Raven Queen extended the Shadowfell into the Fey corrupting the pure emotions of the Fey creatures into mixed beings. They look visually bloody and tear themselves apart in pain.
>There are now numerous dimensional portals where the merging of the planes is at it's strongest. These portals are where the Shadowfell and Fey magic combined into a tear in the cloak of the planes.
>The Splitting of the continents were caused by the oceans of the Fey overtaking the land. The ocean itself is littered with Fey houses and objects. There are Fey islands and remaining land masses from the old world that managed to be higher than the merging waters.
>Material plane and Fey creatures are seperated from each other, some are stuck in Fey and vice versa. The Elves in the material realm are very much resentful of material plane creatures as they naturally blame all of the world on creating a tear.
