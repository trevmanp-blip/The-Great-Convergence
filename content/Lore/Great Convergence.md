
> [!info] In universe
> The world of Teras was a highly advanced world until the merging of the overlapping planes of existence the Shadowfell, Feywild and Material Plane. It all started with a bright flash of green and black flashing across the sky and ground then suddenly creatures. Some torn some otherworldly, the ones with the aura of bloods and tears started ravaging anything close by. The sudden disappearance of people, structures and then the sudden emergence of landscapes, buildings, and creatures merged into each other if unlucky enough to overlap. Not all of the planes were merged but certain central locations named Dimensional Portals, spilled over into the material. Then after the first merge panic the flash went away, only to repeat and appear every year. Due to the mixing planes a new way of telling time had to be invented due to time changing. The Fey Sun marks the first of a new year.
