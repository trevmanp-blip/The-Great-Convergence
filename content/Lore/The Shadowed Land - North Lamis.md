The smallest continent, and the most feared.

When the Feywild began merging here, panic, despair, and cruelty were already widespread. Those emotions corrupted the raw, fast-acting magic of the Feywild, twisting it into something closer to the Shadowfell. North Lamis is now a bleak, undead-haunted land—an echoing sub-realm where negative emotions crash endlessly against Fey magic.

Portals here are unstable, dark, and oppressive. Fey creatures that emerge are warped, hostile, or hollow. Undead are common, and shadows often behave as if alive.

Very few civilizations remain. Those that do survive through isolation, necromancy, or desperate bargains.
