Once home to many peoples, Marel was abandoned almost overnight.

The elven capital, Saret ed Etreum, merged directly onto the coastline of the Material Plane. As Fey structures fused with cities and forests swallowed settlements, panic spread. Most non-elves fled the continent entirely.

When the elves finally regrouped, they blamed the Material Plane for the catastrophe. Outsiders were cast out, borders sealed, and Marel became known as the Sacrificed Land—not because it was destroyed, but because it was given up.

Today, Marel is a vast, forest-covered continent dominated by elven cities and Sylvan creatures. Many Fey beings here are cut off from their original realms, though harnessed portals allow limited travel. Outsiders are rare and closely watched.

Possible origins: elves, Fey-touched travelers, exiles from Marel, diplomats, secret smugglers between planes
