Founding
	Founded 1 GC by Thulk of Southern Treis. Thulk was the grandson of the old kingdom's emperor. The Great Convergence destroyed most of the old kingdom as the hostile and corrupted feywild laid siege to the unknowing cities and capital. Thulk's family slain as they tried to defend the throne and eventually ravaging of the capital left Thulk as a wondering soul. After heading North East closer to the grand mountains he found a group of survivors and eventually created a small village which he named Kret, after his grandfather.  Eventually with his training from the past he was able to defend and grow the future capital of Esther. In 54 GC he started to send out parties of trained soldiers to look for more land and resources which is when they spread out to Lesthelk. With the new farming land, Kret expanded into the eventual districts that it has in present time. 

Present day 203 GC
	Ruled by [[Lothare of The Red Sun - King]] and [[Krathani The Sage - Queen]] they maintain a seemingly stable empire. With how expanded Esther has become they receive word on the worsening state of the Feywild merging with the Material plane. With Krathani desperate to keep her royal bloodline in tact she decided to hide the worsening reports from the public, managing any invasions secretly. The main city is divided in five districts [[Residential]] ,[[Artisan]], [[Academic]], [[Market]], [[City Center]], and [[Political]]. The older buildings are made out of wood with newer building being stone and wood. Richer citizens and nobility typically use more expensive grandeur materials like marble or columns.
