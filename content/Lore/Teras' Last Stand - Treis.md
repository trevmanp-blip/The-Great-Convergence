The largest continent, and the closest thing Teras has to “normal.”

Treis resisted the chaos through rigid control. It is ruled by Esther, a council of descendants from the pre-Merge kingdom. Esther maintains stability through infrastructure, magic, and carefully managed secrecy about how fragile that stability truly is.

Treis contains nearly every biome—plains, forests, mountains, and arctic regions far to the north. The northeastern coast is dominated by a mountain range that cuts inward toward the mainland.

Most citizens live relatively peaceful lives, unaware of how much effort goes into keeping Fey influence contained.
