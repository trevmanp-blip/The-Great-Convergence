In [[Kret - Capital of Esther]], [[Lowell The Human Fighter]] began the campaign, waking up for the first day of "The Day of Salvation" then heading towards the [[Market]]. As he is walking through the populated festival [[Market]] he approaches a makeshift stall made by [[Wizzlebang - WizzleBangs Mystical Enchantments Owner]]. Upon 'offending' Wizzlebang, the kobald threw a firebolt cantrip at his feat and ushers him away. Lowell walks away confused and almost bumps into [[Ping - Rogue Kenku]] who was just simply walking down the market as well. A [[Blink Dog]] being chased by guards blinks in and out of existence that then appears behind Lowell, using his legs to hide. Ping succeeds in tripping one of the guards making it seem like a mistake. The guards give up the chase with the more experienced one complaining about the new trainees. As the guards walk away [[Shimy The Wizard Elven]] walks into the market from outside the city, hood down as she sees the diversity that populates the city and feeling safe being seen. Shimy notices the blink dog in surprise at the presence of another fey creature so far away from home. She catches up to the dog and when questioning who the owner of the dog is, it drags Shimy towards Lowell.

[[Alstroemeria - Bard Fairy]] is the last one introduced as they start hovering in the air near the [[Residential]], [[Market]], and [[City Center]]. Eventually going over to the [[City Center]] with the festival tents. Alstroemeria notices a [[Worg]] in a cage and decides to attempt to free it. First they mistake a woman as the ringleader, creating an illusionary interpretation of her screaming. Guards walk over to investigate while everyone else including the beast masters remain largely uncaring. Upon spotting a torch Alstroemeria dumps oil over the main act tent next to the [[Worg]] cage and using DruidCraft lights the oil on fire creating a quick acting blazing inferno to engulf the cloth. The guards now noticing the fairy run off as eventually they return with more guards and wizards from [[Esther's Guild of Magic and Research]] to help deal with the fire. The fire quickly spreads as the wizards attempts to use create water fails. The guards too busy moving away flammable objects to help save their city is distracted enough not to chase the fairy. As Lowell, Ping, the blink dog, and Shimy move towards the North East entrance way to the [[City Center]] to inspect what is happening they notice the fairy. The blink dog attempts to blink on top of the flying fairy but fails landing underneath on a stall and disappearing. The rest of the party quickly convene where the dog fell noticing the sudden disappearance. Ping convinces the rest of the party to move away towards the city exit but Lowell decides to go to his home to check on his mother [[Natalia - Lowell's mother]]. Natalia already noticing the ever growing fire from her second story window began packing for both her and Lowell. Upon seeing Lowell, feeling relieved she is too focused on surviving to care about the odd company that has followed her son. With her knowledge of the city they swiftly escape with Natalia mentioning moving towards Naor to stay with distant family and that the rest of the party is welcome as she believes they are from [[Kret - Capital of Esther]] and now without a home.

As the players flee from the city in the forested roads leading to Naor they hear a boom in the sky as a red tear ripples through the sky, creating a depthless rift where black-red clouds spill from the tear and begin gathering like clouds. [[Ping - Rogue Kenku]] and [[Shimy The Wizard Elven]] succeeded a perception check to peer into the tear and witnessed a giant eye that shifts as it looks through. After a couple seconds the eye recedes, as it does a green beacon forms beneath it from the ground and extends into the tear past what is visible to the party. Ping cannot form the correct words as he finds his beak unable to open. Shimy decides to keep what she has witnessed to herself not wanting to seem foolish. Natalia expresses that none of this is like anything she has ever seen, which in a way comforts Shimy. Ping sees footprints which is later deduced to be goat hooves and Alstroemeria flies above to see a Satyr cleaning off wounds next to the river. Upon interacting [[Anselm Brode - Satyr found in woods]] communicates he was running from small gremlin like creatures that were covered in thick oil. After some back and forth Shimy notices a streak of the black-red cloud striking down, giving the party enough time to move and prepare. The streak dashes towards Anselm and twists around him, completely covering him but the party can hear wails from the Satyr. As the cloud subsides and lingers in an aura around him they see his body twisted and falling apart, almost rotting. The Satyr oblivious to what is happening is confused and continues to scream, as a streak of the same black-red cloud shoots off of him and into the ground summoning a [[Boggle]] which is seemingly the same creature as described by the Satyr earlier. As the party fights they eventual win as the Boggle is struck down and the Satyr falls apart after Ping stabs where it's heart should be. After striking the heart a burst of the black-red cloud erupts from him and flies back into the sky collecting with the rest of the ominous clouds. The Satyr body almost completely decomposing in a matter of seconds as the cloud's magic seemed to accelerate timer.

--End of session 1--
